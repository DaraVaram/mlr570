1 · Mathematical Background

Everything the rest of the course assumes you already know, gathered in one place — with the reason each piece matters for machine learning attached to it.

PART 1.1

Linear Algebra

Vectors and matrices, multiplication, linear systems, transpose, inverse, rank, null space, matrix families, norms, eigenvalues and SVD.

13 playgrounds
PART 1.2

Probability & Statistics

Sample spaces, discrete and continuous random variables, moments, joint and conditional distributions, Bayes' theorem, independence, sample statistics.

4 playgrounds
PART 1.3

Calculus & Optimisation

Limits and derivatives, gradients, vector and matrix calculus, the chain rule, convexity and gradient descent.

4 playgrounds
PART 1.4

Further Topics

Inner products and orthonormal bases, positive definite matrices, quadratic forms, orthogonal projections, LLN and CLT, and algorithmic complexity.

5 playgrounds
Available now

2 · Data Fundamentals

Everything that happens before you fit a model, and everything that happens after — preparing data, measuring similarity, and judging honestly whether the result works.

PART 2.1

Data Preprocessing

Aggregation, sampling, normalisation, encoding, discretisation, PCA and feature selection.

9 playgrounds
PART 2.2

Similarity Measures

Pearson correlation, Euclidean and Manhattan distance, the curse of dimensionality, cosine and Jaccard similarity.

5 playgrounds
PART 2.3

Model Evaluation

Data splits, over- and under-fitting, k-fold cross-validation, classification and regression metrics, ROC and precision–recall curves.

7 playgrounds
Available now

3 · Supervised Learning I

Linear models and the kernel methods that free them — from the perceptron through support vector machines to kernel ridge regression and k-nearest neighbours.

PARTS 3.1–3.2

Classification & SVMs

Linear separability, the perceptron and its gradient-descent reading, maximum-margin classification, the dual and KKT conditions, soft margins.

3 playgrounds
PART 3.3

Linear Regression

Ordinary and weighted least squares, ridge regression and multicollinearity, Lasso and elastic net.

2 playgrounds
PARTS 3.4–3.5

Kernels & k-NN

Nonlinear feature maps, the kernel trick, polynomial and RBF kernels, kernel least squares, and k-nearest neighbours.

5 playgrounds
Available now

4 · Trees & Neural Networks

Two models with opposite personalities: trees that you can read aloud, and networks that learn their own representation.

PART 4.1

Decision Trees

ID3 and greedy construction, entropy and information gain, the Play Tennis worked example, real-valued splits, Gini and Tsallis, overfitting and pruning.

5 playgrounds
PART 4.2

Feedforward Neural Networks

Architecture and the forward pass, output layers and losses, activation functions, backpropagation derived step by step, optimisers, universal approximation, and a full layer-by-layer XOR run-through.

7 playgrounds
Available now

5 · CNNs, RNNs & Transformers

Three architectures, three different assumptions about which parts of the input belong together: locality, recurrence, and learned attention.

PART 5.1

Convolutional Networks

Images as numbers, the convolution operation and its arithmetic, padding modes, the feature hierarchy, pooling, receptive fields, and LeNet through EfficientNet.

8 playgrounds
PART 5.2

Recurrent Networks

The vanilla RNN with a fully worked forward pass, backpropagation through time, vanishing and exploding gradients, and the LSTM and GRU gating that fix them.

6 playgrounds
PART 5.3

Transformers

Tokens and embeddings, positional encoding, scaled dot-product attention and why the scaling is there, multi-head attention, causal masking and the two pre-training objectives.

7 playgrounds
Available now

6 · Unsupervised Learning

No labels at all — only data, and the question of what structure is worth finding in it.

PARTS 6.1–6.2

Clustering as Optimisation

The WCSS objective and the ANOVA identity, Lloyd's algorithm worked through step by step, where k-means fails and why, k-means++ seeding, k-medoids and kernel k-means.

6 playgrounds
PARTS 6.3–6.4

Density & Soft Clustering

DBSCAN, density-reachability, core/border/noise and the k-distance plot for choosing ε; then Gaussian mixtures, EM, and the limit in which EM becomes k-means.

3 playgrounds
PART 6.5

Autoencoders

The bottleneck and reconstruction loss, the exact equivalence between linear autoencoders and PCA, what nonlinearity buys, denoising and sparse variants, and VAEs.

4 playgrounds
